VOC & Analytics · Mashreq Bank · Dubai, UAE

Building a VOC Engine from Zero

Feedback cycle reduced from weeks to days
The situation

Mashreq Bank had no structured customer feedback infrastructure. Satisfaction data was fragmented across channels, collected manually, and took weeks to reach decision-makers — by which time it was operationally useless. Leadership had no consistent view of CX performance, and product teams were prioritising based on assumption rather than evidence.

What I did

I first designed a bespoke in-house VOC platform to prove the concept, mapping survey logic, triggers, and reporting architecture from scratch. Once validated, I led the full vendor selection, RFP design, and procurement of Qualtrics — coordinating scoring panels across CX, IT, Legal, and Procurement. Post-implementation, I deployed AI-driven text analytics to auto-categorise open-text feedback at scale, and built Power BI dashboards that consolidated NPS, CSAT, complaint trends, and sentiment into a single decision-ready view for C-suite.

Tools & methods
QualtricsPower BI AI text analyticsSentiment analysis RFP designSurvey architecture SAS programming
Outcomes delivered
  • Feedback-to-insight cycle reduced from weeks to days
  • Single source of CX truth adopted by C-suite for quarterly business reviews
  • AI-enabled auto-categorisation of open-text feedback at enterprise scale
  • VOC insights directly drove backlog prioritisation across product and digital squads
  • Reduced manual reporting effort significantly, freeing team capacity for analysis
CX Strategy & Journey Governance · Mashreq Bank · Dubai, UAE

45-Point NPS Transformation

+45 NPS points · 50% reduction in detractors
The situation

NPS scores were declining and end-to-end journey ownership was fragmented across siloed teams. There was no formal governance model, no consistent way to connect customer feedback to delivery decisions, and no mechanism for prioritising journey improvements cross-functionally. Pain points were known anecdotally but never mapped, quantified, or systematically acted upon.

What I did

I developed the bank's first formal CX journey governance model, embedding clear ownership within agile squads and establishing a cross-functional framework for prioritising what mattered most to customers. I applied systems thinking to connect VOC data, survey sequencing, suppression logic, and comms approvals into one coherent end-to-end picture. Journey mapping and discovery workshops identified the highest-impact failure points across HNI, Mortgage, Cards, and Funds Transfer. I led iterative user testing and redesign cycles, presenting journey performance trajectories directly to C-suite and using data to maintain delivery accountability.

Tools & methods
Service blueprintingJourney mapping Agile squad governanceVOC analytics User testingNPS governance framework Power BIStakeholder workshops
Outcomes delivered
  • 45-point NPS increase over three years through targeted journey redesign
  • 50% reduction in customer detractors
  • Cross-team priority disputes reduced approximately 30% through governance model
  • Increased share of customer-centric backlog items funded and delivered
  • CEO's Club Top Contributor: 2019, 2022, and 2023
Digital Transformation · Mashreq Bank · Dubai, UAE

40% Channel Shift in 12 Months

40% reduction in branch & phone demand
The situation

Branch and phone channels were absorbing high volumes of avoidable contacts — queries that could be self-served digitally. This was driving operating cost, creating long wait times, and frustrating customers who expected a modern digital experience. Digital channels existed but hadn't earned customer trust due to friction and incomplete journeys.

What I did

I served as CX lead across end-to-end digital journey design for website, mobile banking, chatbot, ATM, CDM, and IVR. I designed and implemented AI-enabled chatbot and self-service journeys, reducing avoidable call volumes and improving first-contact resolution. I co-created 50+ customer educational videos targeting the top FAQ categories and owned IVR containment rate analysis, redesigning call flows to reduce misrouting. WFM demand data directly informed which digital investments had the highest contact-deflection ROI.

Tools & methods
Chatbot designIVR architecture WFM analyticsUX prototyping Content strategyDigital channel analytics User testing
Outcomes delivered
  • 40% reduction in branch service demand within 12 months
  • Significant reduction in avoidable call volumes to the contact centre
  • 50+ customer educational videos deployed, measurably reducing inbound queries
  • IVR containment rates improved across all retail and business banking segments
  • WFM-led ROI modelling directly shaped digital investment decisions
Strategy & Segmentation · NZ Safety Blackwoods · Auckland, NZ

Uncovering a Hidden $500K Revenue Opportunity

$500K+ annual revenue opportunity quantified
The situation

A large B2B portfolio was organised by revenue tier alone — a blunt lens that masked a growing cohort of high-potential accounts quietly disengaging. Leadership had no visibility into which accounts were at risk, which were underserved, or where relationship investment would generate the best return.

What I did

I designed DeciMatrix — a novel multi-factor segmentation model re-categorising accounts across engagement frequency, product breadth, growth trajectory, and strategic fit, moving well beyond a revenue-only lens. I validated the logic with sales, data, and commercial teams, then ran analysis that pinpointed a previously invisible at-risk segment. A tiered engagement strategy was mapped to each segment, with SOPs for the account management team to execute sustainably. Findings were presented directly to the executive team, securing buy-in for phased rollout.

Tools & methods
Segmentation modellingData analysis BRD writingSOP design Executive presentationCommercial strategy
Outcomes delivered
  • $500K+ annual revenue opportunity identified and quantified
  • New tiered engagement strategy adopted by account management team
  • SOPs and frameworks enabling sustainable, replicable execution post-engagement
  • Executive buy-in secured for phased rollout across the B2B portfolio

About

The person behind the work

I didn't start out wanting to be a "Product Owner." I started out wanting to understand why customers were frustrated, and then figure out how to fix it. Over 15 years, that question has taken me from contact centres in India to the towers of Dubai, and the answer has always been the same: friction in the customer experience is always a system problem, not a surface problem.

At Mashreq Bank, where I spent a decade, I learned what it looks like when CX strategy, data infrastructure, Product and delivery discipline all work together in one room. I designed the VOC platform from scratch, watched those insights drive a 45-point NPS turnaround, built the workforce models that shaped digital investment decisions, wrote the BRDs, ran the user tests, managed the vendors, and coached the analysts. Not because the role required all of it, but because the outcome demanded it.

I've operated inside and alongside digital product squads as the person who sits between the customer, the business, and the technology team, translating VOC data into user stories, conflicting stakeholder priorities into a coherent backlog, and complex delivery realities into clear executive narratives. That's the work I find most energising.

"I'm at my best when the problem is genuinely hard, the data is imperfect, and everyone in the room has a different view of what good looks like."

I'm now based in Auckland, bringing that same full-stack approach to organisations in the NZ market that are serious about treating customer experience as a commercial lever. I hold an MBA from Massey University completed with top academic distinction in 2025, a Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) certification, and a Bachelor of Commerce from Mumbai University.

Outside of work I'm driven by curiosity, exploring how AI is reshaping the product landscape, staying current on agile delivery practices, and simply talking to customers and discovering what they actually need. That curiosity is what keeps the work honest.
